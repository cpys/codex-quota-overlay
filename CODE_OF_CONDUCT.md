# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Codex Quota Overlay a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Community members are expected to be respectful, constructive, patient, and mindful that bug reports can involve managed devices or sensitive work environments. Never pressure another person to share screenshots, logs, account information, private paths, or workplace data.

## Expected behavior

- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Respect privacy boundaries and requests to redact information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its community.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/cpys). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
